Updates & explanations

Refresh is manual by default. Optional scheduling runs only while the app or a cron job is running. The offline app does not update. Optional server-side AI selects relevant stored evidence; it cannot author numerical forecasts or injury claims. Without a key, grounded explanations still work.

Chronological validation uses only statistics available before the target game. Historical news features are excluded. Rest, usage, home/away and opponent adjustments are not enabled without validated improvement.
